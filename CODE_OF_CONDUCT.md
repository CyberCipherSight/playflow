# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository hosting platform's available reporting tools or to a maintainer through a private contact listed on their profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Apply these expectations to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
